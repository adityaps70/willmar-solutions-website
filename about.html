Who we are

Willmar Solutions Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based marine consultancy supporting shipowners, ship managers, charterers, cargo interests and maritime organisations. We combine practical shipboard experience with shore-based expertise in marine operations, HSEQ, navigation, cargo assurance, auditing and training.

We support individual vessels, fleets, cargo movements and specialised marine projects. Assignments may be delivered remotely worldwide or through onboard attendance in selected locations, depending on the operational requirement.

What We Do

We help clients prevent losses, prepare vessels, strengthen navigational safeguards and resolve operational concerns. Our services range from focused remote assignments to detailed onboard inspections, cargo supervision and longer-term consultancy support.

Our Reach

Remote support is available worldwide. Onboard attendance can be arranged through Willmar’s leadership and selected marine professionals in key maritime locations, subject to scope, availability and local requirements.

Our Team

Sea-going experience, shore-based expertise and consistent standards across every assignment.

Willmar Solutions brings together practical maritime knowledge and strong capabilities in business management, marine operations, HSEQ, navigation, auditing, cargo assurance and training. Clients receive direct access to the people responsible for their work.

Ms. Prity Dayal

Founder and Director

Prity is an MBA professional with experience in advertising, human resources and the pharmaceutical sector. She also brings first-hand maritime exposure from sailing onboard vessels for extended periods as a supernumerary, giving her a practical understanding of shipboard life, crew requirements and operating conditions at sea.

She founded Willmar Solutions to provide reliable, responsive and client-focused services to the maritime industry. She leads business development, client coordination, project planning, documentation and service follow-up, ensuring assignments are efficiently managed from enquiry through completion.

Capt. Rajiv Bali

Principal Consultant and Co-founder

Capt. Rajiv Bali is a Master Mariner with more than 35 years of sea-going and shore-based experience across tankers, gas carriers, bulk carriers and container vessels. He brings nearly two decades of shore-based experience in marine operations, HSEQ, auditing, training and consultancy.

He provides technical leadership for navigational assurance, vessel audits and inspections, cargo loss prevention, remote magnetic compass adjustment, incident investigation and maritime training.

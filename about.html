Who We Are

Willmar Solutions Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based marine consultancy supporting Shipowners, Ship Managers, Charterers, Cargo interests, Classification societies and Maritime Organisations.

We combine practical seagoing experience with shore-based expertise in Marine Operations, Safety, Navigation, Cargo Assurance, Vessel Inspections, Compass Adjustment and Maritime Training.

Our support is available remotely worldwide, with onboard attendance arranged in selected maritime locations according to the assignment requirements.

We help clients identify operational risks, prevent avoidable losses, prepare vessels for inspections and strengthen navigational and cargo safeguards.

Our services range from focused remote support to onboard inspections, cargo supervision, marine consultancy and customised training.

Our Team

Willmar Solutions combines practical maritime knowledge with experience in business management, marine operations, HSEQ, navigation, auditing, cargo assurance and training.

Ms. Prity Dayal

Founder and Director

Prity is an MBA professional with experience in advertising, human resources and the pharmaceutical sector. She also brings first-hand maritime exposure from spending extended periods sailing onboard vessels as a supernumerary, giving her a practical understanding of shipboard life and operating conditions at sea. She founded Willmar Solutions to provide reliable, responsive and client-focused services to the maritime industry.

She leads Willmar’s business development, client coordination, project planning, documentation and service follow-up, ensuring that assignments are efficiently managed from enquiry through completion.

Capt. Rajiv Bali

Principal Consultant and Co-founder

Capt. Rajiv Bali is a Master Mariner with more than 36 years of seagoing and shore-based experience across Tankers, Gas Carriers, Bulk Carriers and Container Vessels.

With nearly two decades of shore-based experience in Marine Operations, HSEQ, Training and Consultancy, he provides technical leadership across Willmar’s Navigational Assurance, Vessel Audits and Inspections, Cargo Loss Prevention, Magnetic Compass Adjustment, Incident Investigation and Maritime Training Services.

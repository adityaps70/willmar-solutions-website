Protect cargo integrity, reduce avoidable loss and maintain better control from loading through discharge.

Overview

Willmar Solutions provides independent cargo assurance and loss-prevention support for tanker, bulk, bagged and breakbulk operations. We identify material risks early, monitor critical activities and coordinate practical intervention before problems develop into damage, shortage, contamination, delay or dispute.

Oil Tankers

Our tanker specialists bring extensive experience in safety, cargo operations and loss control. We support the planning and execution of loading, discharge and ship-to-ship operations, with close attention to tank readiness, cargo quantity and quality, ship–shore coordination, procedures, documentation and the timely investigation of discrepancies.

  • Loading and discharge planning and supervision
  • Ship-to-ship operational support
  • Cargo quantity and loss-control monitoring
  • Tank readiness and contamination-risk checks
  • Ship–shore coordination and documentation review
  • Operational discrepancy and incident support

Bulk Carriers and Breakbulk Cargoes

We support vessel-suitability assessment, cargo-hold readiness, hatch-cover integrity, safe handling, stowage, segregation, weather precautions and cargo-condition monitoring. Our approach connects the physical condition of the vessel with the practical risks present during loading, carriage and discharge.

Specialised PTA Cargo Support

Willmar Solutions has specialist experience in the carriage of Purified Terephthalic Acid in jumbo bags. Our end-to-end support covers pre-loading risk assessment, hold and hatch readiness, bilge safeguards, weather monitoring, loading and stowage supervision, moisture and contamination controls, voyage precautions, discharge attendance and cargo-condition reporting.

The objective is to reduce wetting, staining, bag damage, spillage and contamination while providing cargo interests with timely evidence and practical control throughout the shipment.

Remote Magnetic Compass Adjustment

One properly planned vessel swing can reduce deviation, fuel use and avoidable disruption.

Plan a Remote Compass Adjustment
Overview

Willmar Solutions provides professional remote magnetic compass adjustment to vessels worldwide. Experienced Master Mariners and compass adjustors guide the vessel’s Master and bridge team through a structured process designed to complete adjustment during a single 360-degree swing under suitable conditions.

At a glance

Delivery
Remote, worldwide
Method
One controlled 360° swing, eight headings
Target
Residual deviation within 3° where conditions permit
Plan a Remote Compass Adjustment

How the Service Works

  1. 01We review compass details, photographs, vessel information and planned swing conditions.
  2. 02The vessel records observations on eight headings during one controlled swing.
  3. 03We analyse the readings and give clear, step-by-step instructions for the required corrections.
  4. 04Post-adjustment readings are checked and an updated deviation card is issued when satisfactory.
  5. 05Technical support remains available after completion, subject to the agreed scope.

Why Vessel Managers Use the Service

  • Reduced dependence on arranging a physical adjustor at port
  • Less vessel time, fuel consumption and schedule disruption
  • Direct support during planning and execution of the swing
  • Adjustment targeted to achieve residual deviation within 3 degrees, where conditions and compass condition permit
  • Experience supporting more than 1,000 vessel adjustments through our lead compass adjustor
Remote adjustment remains subject to suitable vessel, compass, weather, sea-area and operational conditions. The Master retains command of the vessel and determines whether the swing can be conducted safely.

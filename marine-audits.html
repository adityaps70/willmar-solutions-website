Overview

Willmar Solutions supports shipowners and ship managers in assessing vessel condition, operational practices, crew readiness and compliance before deficiencies lead to inspection observations, detention, incidents or commercial disruption.

Our audits and inspections are led by experienced Master Mariners and marine professionals. Reports distinguish material risks from minor observations and provide workable recommendations—not merely a completed checklist.

Our Services

Pre-purchase Inspections

Independent assessment of vessel condition, compliance and operational risk to support an informed acquisition decision.

Post-takeover Inspections

Structured assessment after takeover to establish priorities and support safe integration into the new management system.

Navigational Audits

Review of bridge procedures, passage planning, ECDIS use, watchkeeping, bridge-team management and SMS implementation.

VDR Analysis

Review of available voyage data to assess navigation, bridge-team performance, equipment use, communications and procedural compliance.

Pre-SIRE 2.0 Assessments

Independent readiness support focused on crew familiarity, human factors, equipment condition and operational execution. This is not an OCIMF SIRE inspection.

Pre-RightShip Assessment

Gap analysis and preparation to help vessel and shore teams address deficiencies before the formal RightShip inspection.

PSC and AMSA Preparation

Remote or onboard review of statutory compliance, equipment readiness, documentation and crew preparedness before higher-risk calls.

ISM and SMS Audits

Assessment of whether the Safety Management System is understood, implemented and effective onboard.

Vessel and Cargo-Space Inspections

Condition and suitability checks covering cargo holds, hatch covers, watertight integrity, bilges and related cargo systems.

Incident Investigation and Root-Cause Analysis

Structured examination of incidents, near misses and operational failures to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence.

Support Beyond the Report

Where included in the assignment, we assist vessel and shore teams in understanding findings, reviewing corrective evidence and preparing for effective close-out. Services can be arranged for one vessel, a fleet programme, a focused remote review or onboard attendance.

Pre-SIRE 2.0 and pre-RightShip assessments are independent readiness services provided by Willmar Solutions. They are not official OCIMF SIRE or RightShip inspections and do not replace them.

Marine Consultancy Services

Direct access to experienced marine professionals when an operation, incident or project requires informed judgement.

Overview

Willmar Solutions provides independent, practical consultancy to help shipowners, ship managers, charterers and cargo interests control risk, resolve operational concerns and protect their safety and commercial objectives.

At a glance

Delivery
Remote or onboard, by assignment
For
Shipowners, managers, charterers and cargo interests
Scope
Operations, incidents, charters and projects
Discuss a Marine Assignment

Consultancy Capabilities

Port captaincy and operational attendance

On-site coordination and practical oversight during critical vessel and cargo operations.

Ship-to-ship transfer supervision

Operational support focused on planning, readiness, coordination and safe execution.

Vessel condition and suitability assessment

Independent technical and operational insight for chartering, acquisition or deployment decisions.

Incident investigation and root-cause analysis

Evidence-based review with corrective and preventive recommendations.

Ship brokerage and post-fixture support

Commercial and operational coordination from fixture through performance of the voyage.

Pre- and post-charter assistance

Focused support for vessel readiness, documentation, cargo requirements and operational follow-through.

Safety Management System review and simplification

Practical review aimed at improving usability, ownership and onboard implementation.

Marine project planning and coordination

Structured management of specialist assignments involving vessels, terminals, surveyors and shore teams.

Remote or Onboard

Support may be provided remotely or onboard, depending on the assignment, urgency, vessel location and local requirements.

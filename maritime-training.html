Maritime Training

Training designed to improve performance onboard—not simply to complete a requirement.

Discuss Your Training Requirement
Overview

Willmar Solutions develops and delivers practical maritime training for sea-going and shore-based personnel. Our programmes combine regulatory requirements, company procedures, operational experience and lessons from real incidents to build knowledge that can be applied at work.

At a glance

Delivery
Classroom, online or onboard
Audience
Sea-going and shore-based personnel
Format
Session, workshop, vessel-specific or company programme
Discuss Your Training Requirement

Training Built Around Your Risks

Programmes can be customised around vessel type, company procedures, audit findings, recurring deficiencies, new requirements or a defined competency gap. Delivery is available in the classroom, online or onboard.

Training Areas

  • Bridge procedures, passage planning and ECDIS practices
  • Bridge Resource Management and navigational risk awareness
  • Tanker, bulk and specialised cargo operations
  • Cargo loss prevention and claims awareness
  • Marine audits, inspection readiness and effective close-out
  • Incident investigation, root-cause analysis and learning from events
  • Safety Management System implementation and practical HSEQ leadership
  • Bespoke technical programmes and animated training content

For Sea and Shore Teams

Training can be delivered as a focused session, workshop, vessel-specific intervention or structured company programme. Content and depth are agreed for the intended audience so that the learning remains relevant, credible and usable.

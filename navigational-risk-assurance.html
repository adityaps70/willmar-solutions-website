Overview

A passage plan can appear complete while still containing unsafe waypoints, unsuitable safety settings, inadequate under-keel clearance or overlooked chart information. Willmar Solutions provides an independent Master Mariner review before the route is approved and implemented, giving vessel and shore teams time to correct deficiencies at the planning stage.

Passage Plan Preparation and Review

We prepare comprehensive port-to-port passage plans or conduct an in-depth review of plans prepared by the vessel’s navigating officers. Identified concerns are reported with practical recommendations, and the prepared or vetted plan is shared with the vessel’s Master.

Before implementation, the Master verifies and may amend the plan according to the vessel’s characteristics, draught, manoeuvring performance, operational requirements, prevailing conditions and professional judgement. The Master retains overriding authority and responsibility for approval and safe execution of the passage.

What We Review

Route construction

Berth-to-berth completeness, waypoint safety, cross-track limits, wheel-over positions, restricted waters, no-go areas, abort points and contingency anchorages.

ECDIS configuration

Safety depth and contour, shallow and deep contours, look-ahead settings, alarms, isolated dangers and route-check results.

Under-keel and overhead clearance

Draught, squat, trim, heel, water density, tide, company limits, bridges, cables and other vertical restrictions.

Charts and publications

ENC coverage and updates, Notices to Mariners, navigational warnings, chart notes, cautions, sailing directions and survey-data limitations.

Tides, currents and weather

Tidal windows, cross-current effects, seasonal conditions, heavy weather and areas of overfalls, eddies or breakers.

Position monitoring

Parallel indexes, clearing and danger bearings, radar targets, fixing methods, echo-sounder use and Master call criteria.

Pilotage and contingencies

Pilot boarding, reporting points, VTS requirements, tug use, critical turns, Master–Pilot Exchange and alternative action.

Standards and Vessel Requirements

Each assignment considers applicable international regulations, the Company’s Safety Management System and relevant SIRE 2.0 and RightShip RISQ expectations. The review also checks that critical information is clearly reflected on the ECDIS and that amendments are route-checked, briefed and reapproved.

Independent Oversight Without Replacing the Bridge Team

Our service adds a structured shore-side layer of review. It does not replace the vessel’s appraisal, planning, execution and monitoring responsibilities, the navigating officers’ checks, or the Master’s final approval and professional judgement.

What the Client Receives

  • A prepared port-to-port route or an independent review of the vessel’s route
  • A structured record of deficiencies and recommended corrections
  • Verification of critical ECDIS, UKC, monitoring and contingency elements
  • A reviewed plan for the Master’s verification, amendment and final approval

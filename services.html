03 / 09

Marine Audits, Inspections, Surveys & Vessel Readiness

Identify operational, safety and compliance gaps before they result in inspection findings, detention, incidents or commercial disruption. We provide independent assessments and practical vessel-readiness support, with clear priorities for corrective action.

Delivery
Remote support while the vessel is at sea or onboard attendance, depending on the assignment.
Scope of Services

Audits

  • ISM Audits
  • Dynamic Navigational Assessments and VDR Audit
  • Cargo Operations Audits
  • Mooring and Anchoring Operations Audits
  • Engine Room and Machinery Operations Audits
  • MARPOL and Environmental Compliance Audits

Inspections

  • Pre-Purchase and Post-Takeover Inspections
  • Pre-Charter Condition and Suitability Inspections
  • On-Hire and Off-Hire Condition Inspections

Surveys

  • Bunker quantity and ROB surveys, including on-hire, off-hire, takeover and handover surveys
  • Pre-Loading Cargo Hold Inspections and Surveys

Vessel Readiness

  • Pre-SIRE 2.0 and Pre-RightShip Assessments
  • PSC, AMSA and USCG Readiness Reviews
  • Remote Inspection Preparation and Corrective Action Follow-Up
Follow-Up Support
We remain available to review photographs, documents, short videos and corrective evidence, supported by telephone calls and Microsoft Teams meetings, to assist the vessel and shore team through effective close-out.

Navigational Risk Assurance – Remote Passage Planning and ECDIS Review

Identify passage-planning and ECDIS deficiencies before the voyage begins, allowing the vessel’s bridge team to correct them at the planning stage.

Delivery
Remote shore support before implementation onboard
Scope
  • Port-to-port passage-plan preparation
  • Independent passage-plan review
  • ECDIS route and safety-setting review
  • UKC and no-go-area verification
  • Remote VDR-based navigational review
  • In-port or high-risk passage assessment
Review Basis
Applicable international requirements, official charts and publications, Company SMS, vessel-specific operational requirements, and relevant SIRE 2.0 and RightShip RISQ expectations.

05 / 09

Magnetic Compass Adjustment – Remote and Onboard

Complete the adjustment through one properly planned vessel swing, helping reduce vessel time, fuel consumption and operational disruption.

Delivery
Remote support worldwide, with onboard attendance available in selected locations.
Method
Only one single controlled 360° vessel swing on eight headings
Target
Residual deviation within 3°.
